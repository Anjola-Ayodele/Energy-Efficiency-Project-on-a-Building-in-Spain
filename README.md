# Energy-Efficiency-Project-on-a-Building-in-Spain
This project explores the energy performance of a commercial building in Spain using an hourly dataset that includes energy consumption, degree days, and other environmental variables.  Examining how different factors influence building energy use and developing methods to evaluate its efficiency and forecast future demand or behaviour.
# Project Goals

# Understand energy drivers

1. Analyse how weather conditions, temperature, and degree days affect energy consumption.

2. Examine how operational patterns influence demand during different hours of the day.

# Normalise energy consumption

1. Use Heating Degree Days (HDD) and Cooling Degree Days (CDD) to adjust energy data.

2. Separate weather-driven consumption from operational inefficiencies.

# Identify patterns and anomalies

1. Detect unusual spikes or drops in energy use.

2. Highlight periods where the building may be operating inefficiently.

3. Examine daily and seasonal patterns.

# Develop a prediction model

1. Build a machine learning model to forecast energy demand.

2. Use historical data and weather indicators to predict future consumption.

3. Evaluate model performance using common regression metrics.
